Logbook

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026  ·  Applies to: Logbook iOS & Android app

1 Who We Are

Logbook is developed and operated by Diogo Marques. If you have any questions about this policy or your personal data, please contact us at diogo.marques@catapult-agency.com.

2 What Data We Collect

We only collect data you explicitly provide. No background tracking, advertising identifiers, or analytics SDKs are used.

Account data (required if you sign in):

  • Email address (via Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In, or manual entry)
  • Display name

Flight log data (required for core functionality):

  • Flight details you add: flight code, route, date, airline, seat class, duration, distance
  • Trip groupings and names you create
  • Optional cover photos you choose from your photo library for trip cards

Optional profile data (only if you choose to enter it):

  • Nationality
  • Home airport code
  • Frequent flyer number
  • Preferred airline and seat preference

Location (one-time, on request only):

  • Coarse device location — used solely to suggest your nearest airport when you tap “use my location” on the add-flight screen. It is never stored, never transmitted to our servers, and discarded immediately after the suggestion is shown.

Beta feedback (only if you submit it):

  • Free-text message, category tag, app version, and platform (iOS/Android). Your name and email are included only if you are signed in at the time of submission.
None of the optional profile fields are required. You can use Logbook fully without providing them. Location is never accessed without an explicit tap action from you.

3 How We Use Your Data

  • To store and sync your flight log across your devices when you are signed in
  • To compute statistics shown in your profile (total miles, airports visited, etc.)
  • To send local flight reminder notifications — only if you enable them
  • To restore your data if you reinstall the app
  • To improve the app during beta testing (feedback submissions only)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4 Third-Party Services

Logbook uses the following third-party services, each subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Sign-In — Apple Privacy Policy. Apple may share a name and email address with us when you sign in. You can choose to hide your email using Apple’s relay service.
  • Google Sign-In — Google Privacy Policy. Google shares your name and email address with us when you sign in.
  • Expo / EAS (Expo Application Services) — Used to build and deliver the app. Expo does not receive your personal flight data. See Expo Privacy Policy.

5 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on a secured server database. Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. We apply industry-standard security headers and rate limiting on all API endpoints.

If you use Logbook as a guest (without an account), all your data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.

6 Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile → Delete Account & Data option in the app. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

7 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including: access to your data, correction, deletion, and data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at diogo.marques@catapult-agency.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8 Children’s Privacy

Logbook is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
