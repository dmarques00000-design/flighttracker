## 2. What Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Data
When you sign in with **Apple Sign-In** or **Google Sign-In**, we receive:
- Your **name** (first and last)
- Your **email address**
- A unique **user identifier** from Apple or Google

We store this to create and maintain your Logbook account. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

### 2.2 Flight & Trip Data
All flight logs and trips you create are stored on our servers linked to your account:
- Flight codes, airline, aircraft type
- Departure and arrival airports and dates
- Seat number, cabin class, travel purpose
- Trip names, notes, and associated flights

This data is entered entirely by you and is used only to power the app's features.

### 2.3 Device & Usage Data
We do **not** collect:
- Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV)
- Location data
- Analytics or crash reports sent to third parties
- Advertising data of any kind

## 3. How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Data Used | Legal Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Create and manage your account | Name, email, user ID | Contract performance |
| Store your flight and trip logs | Flight/trip records | Contract performance |
| Respond to support requests | Email address | Legitimate interest |
| Improve app reliability | Anonymised error logs (server-side only) | Legitimate interest |

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except:
- **Apple / Google** — for authentication only, governed by their own privacy policies
- **Neon (PostgreSQL hosting)** — stores your data in encrypted databases; subject to their DPA
- **Legal obligation** — if required by applicable law or court order

---

## 5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:
- All flight logs and trip records are permanently deleted within **30 days**
- Your authentication identifiers are removed immediately
- Anonymised aggregate statistics may be retained indefinitely

---

## 6. Security

- All data is transmitted over **HTTPS/TLS**
- Passwords are never stored — we use OAuth 2.0 (Apple/Google) only
- Database access is restricted to the application server
- We do not store boarding pass images or wallet passes server-side

## 7. Your Rights

**GDPR (EEA / Switzerland / UK):**
- Right to access your data
- Right to correct inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing

**CCPA (California):**
- Right to know what data is collected
- Right to delete your data
- Right to opt out of sale (we do not sell data)

To exercise any of these rights, email **privacy@logbookapp.com** with the subject line "Privacy Request".

## 8. Children's Privacy
