Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I add a flight? — Tap the + button on the main screen and enter your flight code and date. Logbook will look up the details automatically.
  • Can I import flights from my calendar? — Yes. Go to Settings → Calendar Sync to scan your iOS Calendar for events containing flight codes.
  • Can I import boarding passes? — Yes. Tap Scan Boarding Pass on the add-flight screen to scan a PDF or image from your files.
  • How do I delete a flight? — Open the flight detail view and scroll to the bottom, or swipe left on the flight in the list.
  • Is my data backed up? — If you are signed in, your data is synced to your account and restored automatically if you reinstall the app.
  • Does Logbook work offline? — You can view your existing logbook offline. Adding new flights or enriching flight data requires an internet connection.
  • How do I delete my account? — Go to Profile → Delete Account & Data. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
  • Why does the app ask for calendar access? — Only when you use the Calendar Sync feature. Logbook reads your calendar on-device to look for flight codes; your calendar data is never sent to our servers.
  • Why does the app ask for camera access? — Only when you scan a boarding pass. The camera is never used in the background.
