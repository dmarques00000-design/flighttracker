Privacy Policy

Logbook App  ·  Last updated: March 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Logbook (“the App”). This Privacy Policy explains how Logbook App (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our iOS application. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is:
Logbook App
Contact: diogo.marques@catapult-agency.com

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide the service:

  • Flight data you enter — flight codes, dates, routes, and related travel details you manually add or import via Apple Wallet passes.
  • Account information — if you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique user identifier and, optionally, your name and email address as provided by those services.
  • Device permissions — the App may request access to your photo library (to save share cards) and location (to assist with airport detection). These are optional and only used for the stated purpose.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, sell your data, or track you across other apps or websites.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To display and organise your personal flight logbook
  • To look up flight details via our server-side API
  • To authenticate your account securely
  • To generate and share flight summary cards at your request

5. Data Storage

Your flight data is stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage (AsyncStorage). Account authentication tokens are managed by Apple and Google's respective secure frameworks. We do not maintain a persistent server-side copy of your personal flight logbook.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Sign in with Apple — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Sign-In — policies.google.com/privacy
  • pics.avs.io — used to fetch airline logo images by IATA code. No personal data is transmitted.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your flight data lives on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or remove individual flights within the App. To request deletion of any account-linked data, contact us at diogo.marques@catapult-agency.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights (GDPR / Swiss nDSG)

If you are located in the EU or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact diogo.marques@catapult-agency.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
